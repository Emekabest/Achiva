Delete Your Achiva Account

If you would like to delete your Achiva account and request deletion of associated personal information, you can submit a deletion request by contacting us at:

Email: joetivity@gmail.com

What to include in your request

Please send your request from the email address associated with your Achiva account and include a clear request to delete your account.

What happens after your request

We will review your request and take reasonable steps to delete your Achiva account and associated account information.

Tasks and goals that are stored locally on your device are not stored on our servers. You can remove this locally stored information by deleting the app or clearing its data from your device.

Some information may be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate security or legal purposes.

For more information about how Achiva handles information, please review our Privacy Policy.
